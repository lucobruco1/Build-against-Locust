      How this works

      You

      • Break natural blocks to mine stone/dirt/wood. You can only remove blocks you placed if they were placed by you.
      • Place blocks to close the shell: walls first, then the roof. The Bases panel is literally the number your bots are rewarded for.
      • Bots can only delete their own placed blocks — so nobody fixes your mistakes.

      The hunt

      • At 0:00 the Locust spawns. It navigates with A* over the same voxel grid, breaking blocks in its way.
      • It grabs you (jump to struggle), then stabs. Death is temporary: everyone revives when the 3 minutes are up.
      • Sealed + roofed = it must chew through. Leave a gap and it comes in.

      The learning

      • Each brain: representation net → recurrent dynamics with a value-prefix head → policy/value heads, trained with a 5-step latent unroll, SimSiam consistency, two-hot categorical value & reward heads.
      • Actions come from MCTS (PUCT, Dirichlet root noise) over the learned latent model; the visit distribution is the policy target.
      • Replay is prioritised per game; stale samples get SVE (stored search values) instead of TD targets; off-policy depth shrinks with priority.
      • An expert prior acts for a share of decisions (decaying, with a floor) and its distribution is the imitation target — that is why the bots can build at all in cycle 1.
      • Progress is saved to .data/brains.json: shut the server down and the bots are still smarter tomorrow.
